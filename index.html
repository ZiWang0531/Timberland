PROJECT NAME

Timberland
Ad

Enabling accessible entertainment through standard subtitles, SDH (Subtitling for Deaf and Hard of Hearing), and AD (Audio Descriptions), aiming to empower inclusivity.

Overview

This media translation project was driven by the core goal of inclusivity, aiming to enable individuals with disabilities to engage in life and entertainment just like ordinary people through comprehensive subtitling and audio description strategies for a Timberland advertisement.

Multiple Formats

Edited and refined subtitle translations, delivering the project in Standard English and Chinese subtitles, SDH (Subtitling for Deaf and Hard of Hearing), and AD.

Audio Description (AD)

Reviewed subtitle quality, meticulously edited audio description scripts, and provided professional voiceovers to bring the visuals to life.
