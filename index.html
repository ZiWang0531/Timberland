MEDIA TRANSLATION (09/2023 - 10/2023)

Timberland
Ad

Enabling accessible entertainment through standard subtitles, SDH, and Audio Descriptions.

Overview

This media translation project was driven by the core goal of inclusivity—aiming to enable individuals with disabilities to engage in life and entertainment just like ordinary people through comprehensive subtitling and audio description strategies for a Timberland advertisement.

Multiple Formats

Edited and refined subtitle translations, delivering the project in Standard English and Chinese subtitles, SDH (Subtitling for Deaf and Hard of Hearing), and AD.

Audio Description (AD)

Reviewed subtitle quality, meticulously edited audio description scripts, and provided professional voiceovers to bring the visuals to life.

01 — ACCESSIBILITY

Technical Execution

Ensuring precise timing for SDH subtitles and perfectly paced voiceovers for the Audio Descriptions, transforming standard commercial media into an inclusive experience.

Impact & Value

Breaking down communication barriers.

Visual Inclusivity

Crafting descriptive scripts that accurately portray actions and expressions for visually impaired audiences.

Auditory Accessibility

Developing SDH subtitles that capture not just dialogue, but critical sound effects and tonal shifts.

Voiceover Delivery

Providing seamless voiceover work that fits naturally within the gaps of the original advertisement audio.
